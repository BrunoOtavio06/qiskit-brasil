## IBM Quantum Composition & Layout

Three compositional approaches govern IBM Quantum layouts:

### Essential
For scientific information, editorial content, and simple data callouts.
- Form follows function — nothing is decorative or gratuitous
- Ample negative space opens up the design
- Clarity of design delivers the message or goal
- Structure remains simple until additional complexity is needed

### Modular
For organizing, communicating, and scaling different content types in a systematic, human-centered way. Inspired by Carbon Design System cards.
- Spot colors highlight key information
- Rounded corners (nod to the circle/continuum element) add humanity — reserved for expressive use: posters, social posts, films, infographics, illustrations, signage, presentations
- Do NOT round corners on Carbon Design System components
- Systematic logic rules all decisions — nothing is arbitrary
- Elements align to each other and the underlying 2x grid
- Measurements, shapes, ratios, and proportions repeat for consistency

**Grid/spacing variants:**
| Gutter | Use case |
|--------|----------|
| 4px    | Dashboards, presentations, data visualizations (default tight) |
| 8px    | Large formats or when more space is needed between modules |
| 16px (narrow) | Web and digital products (Carbon narrow grid) |
| 32px (wide)   | Web and digital products (Carbon wide grid) |
| 1px (condensed) | Carbon condensed grid |

### Dynamic
For impactful posters, covers, large lead spaces, and expressive callouts. Represents the non-static nature of quantum mechanics.
- Placement always aligns to the underlying 2x grid
- Positive and negative space work together in asymmetric balance
- Asymmetric layouts create qualities of space and depth
- Compositions elicit a sense of movement and vitality
- Expectations are broken to surprise and delight
- Experimentation and playfulness are apparent
